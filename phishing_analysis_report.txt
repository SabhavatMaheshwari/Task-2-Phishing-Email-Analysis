TASK 2: PHISHING EMAIL ANALYSIS

Objective

To identify phishing characteristics in a suspicious Microsoft-themed email sample.

1. SUSPICIOUS SENDER DOMAIN

The email claims to be from Microsoft, but the sender's email address does not use an official Microsoft domain. This indicates possible sender impersonation or spoofing.

2. SCARE / URGENT MESSAGE

The email claims that the Microsoft account password was changed and that the account has been compromised. This creates fear and encourages the recipient to act quickly.

3. MISLEADING TECHNICAL DETAILS

The email provides technical-looking information such as the location, browser, and IP address associated with the alleged account activity. These details can make the email appear genuine and increase the recipient's concern.

4. SUSPICIOUS PASSWORD-RESET LINK

The email asks the recipient to click a "Reset your password" link after claiming that the account has been compromised. This is a key phishing indicator because it encourages the recipient to follow a link provided in the suspicious email.

CONCLUSION

The analyzed email contains several phishing indicators, including a suspicious sender domain, scare/urgent messaging, misleading technical details, and a password-reset link. These characteristics demonstrate common phishing and social-engineering techniques.

SAFETY MEASURES

- Do not click suspicious links.
- Verify the sender independently.
- Do not provide passwords or sensitive information.
- Report suspected phishing emails.
