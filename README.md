TASK 2 – PHISHING EMAIL ANALYSIS

Objective

To identify phishing characteristics in a suspicious Microsoft-themed email sample.

EMAIL ANALYSIS

The sample email was examined for phishing indicators including sender information, urgency, technical details, and suspicious links.

PHISHING INDICATORS FOUND

1. Suspicious Sender Domain
The email claims to be from Microsoft, but the sender's email address does not use an official Microsoft domain.

2. Scare / Urgent Message
The email claims that the Microsoft account password was changed and that the account has been compromised. This creates fear and encourages quick action.

3. Misleading Technical Details
The email provides technical-looking information such as location, browser, and IP address associated with the alleged account activity.

4. Suspicious Password-Reset Link
The email asks the recipient to click a "Reset your password" link after claiming that the account has been compromised.

CONCLUSION

The email contains several phishing indicators, including a suspicious sender domain, scare messaging, misleading technical details, and a password-reset link.

SAFETY MEASURES

- Do not click suspicious links.
- Verify the sender independently.
- Do not provide passwords or sensitive information.
- Report suspected phishing emails.
